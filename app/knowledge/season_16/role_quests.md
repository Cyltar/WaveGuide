# Season 16 Role Quests

Last updated: September 2026
Applies to: Standard Summoner's Rift
Primary sources: Riot Games Patch 26.1, 26.9, 26.11, and 26.16 notes

# Quick Reference

- Role Quests are tied to the player's assigned role and reward normal role-appropriate gameplay.
- Top, Mid, Bot, Jungle, and Support each have distinct quest progression rules and rewards.
- Bot quest completion requires 1,350 quest points.
- Quest progress can come from actions such as gaining lane experience, killing minions, champion takedowns, and participating in turret plate/turret pressure, depending on the role.
- Leaving the intended lane for extended periods can reduce or stop efficient quest progression.
- Support roaming is no longer effectively free: Patch 26.16 increased the importance of remaining near Bot lane during early quest progression.
- Early roaming should therefore account for the quest progress sacrificed by leaving lane, not only the immediate value of the roam.
- Quest completion creates an important macro power spike; compare remaining quest progress against the value of abandoning lane for an objective, roam, recall, or cross-map play.
- For exact point values, thresholds, role-specific progression rules, and rewards, use the detailed sections below.

# Overview

Season 16 introduced assigned Role Quests for Top, Mid, and Bot while updating the existing Jungle and Support quest systems.

A player's quest is determined by their assigned role in champion select.

If players swap positions, they should officially swap roles during champion select so the correct Role Quest is assigned.

Top, Mid, and Bot complete their quests by accumulating quest points.

Jungle completes its quest by feeding treats to the jungle pet.

Support completes its quest through the World Atlas / Runic Compass support-item quest.

# Important Current Quest-Lane Rules

These rules were significantly changed in Patch 26.9.

## Passive Progress

Starting at 1:05:

Outside the assigned quest lane:
- 0.333 quest points per second
- approximately 1 point every 3 seconds
- approximately 20 points per minute

While in the assigned quest lane:
- 1.5 quest points per second
- approximately 90 points per minute

This means remaining in the assigned lane generates approximately:

70 additional quest points per minute

compared with being outside the lane.

## What Counts as "In the Quest Lane"

As of Patch 26.9:

"In the quest lane" means anywhere within the assigned lane outside the player's base.

Before Patch 26.9, passive progress was tied much more closely to the area around / beyond the outermost living turret.

Do NOT use the old outer-turret-area definition.

---

# Roaming Time Bank

Patch 26.9 added a system specifically to allow normal roams without destroying quest progression.

After level 3:

- Spending time in the quest lane banks roaming time.
- 2 seconds spent in lane banks approximately 1 second of roaming time.
- Maximum stored roaming time: 60 seconds.

Therefore:

120 seconds in lane
=
60 seconds of banked roaming time

While consuming banked roaming time outside the assigned lane, the player continues receiving the increased in-lane passive progression rate.

This means a player can roam for up to approximately 60 seconds without losing passive quest progress if they have fully banked roaming time.

## Macro Implication

Do NOT tell players that they must permanently stay in lane until their quest is finished.

A better rule is:

- establish lane presence
- bank roaming time
- push the wave
- use the banked window for meaningful roams or objective rotations
- return before unnecessarily sacrificing waves and quest progress

---

# Recall Penalty

Patch 26.9 changed how recalling interacts with passive quest progression.

After recalling:

Passive quest point generation is disabled for:

12 seconds

This prevents the shorter mid lane from gaining a small unintended advantage from recall travel time.

---

# Out-of-Lane Action Penalty

Patch 26.9 increased the early penalty for earning quest progress from:

- minion kills
- turret takedowns
- turret plates

outside the assigned quest lane.

At low quest progress, the penalty can be as high as:

-75%

The penalty decreases toward:

0%

as the quest gets closer to completion.

Therefore the listed lane-specific values below represent the maximum / intended value when performing the action in the assigned lane.

Early in the quest, taking farm, plates, or turrets in another lane is much less efficient for quest completion.

Champion takedowns and epic-monster takedowns are not listed as part of this Patch 26.9 CS/turret/plate penalty.

The quest strongly rewards:

- lane XP
- minion farming
- turret pressure
- plates
- remaining within top lane long enough to bank roam time

→ participate in the fight

15 points

90 passive points per minute

Mid is the only lane quest that directly gains progress from damaging enemy champions.

1.5% of champion damage dealt

Melee champions:

3% of champion damage dealt

Example:

Deal 1000 champion damage:

1000 × 0.03
=
30 quest points

Therefore melee Mid champions earn twice as much quest progress per point of champion damage as ranged Mid champions.

The support progresses the item by generating gold through Shared Riches.

# Patch 26.16 — Major Support Roaming Nerf

Patch 26.16 specifically targeted supports behaving like a second jungler very early in the game.

Riot increased the cost of leaving Bot lane early.

## Outside-Bot Minion Penalty

Before 26.16:

-25% minion gold and XP outside Bot lane
until level 3

Current:

-33% minion gold and XP outside Bot lane
until level 5

This is a significant early-roaming penalty.

Do NOT use the old:

"25% until level 3"

rule.

Current rule:

33% reduction
until level 5

outside Bot lane.

---

# Support Shared Riches — Patch 26.16

Patch 26.16 also removed the advantage that champion / turret harassment had over minion execution for early quest progression.

Before the patch, champion and turret damage could generate more quest value than taking CS.

The system was changed so the two methods are much closer / equalized.

Current stack-consumption values introduced in 26.16:

First relevant stage:
18 gold

Later relevant stage:
21 gold

for both:

- champion / turret damage
- minion CS execution

The important gameplay consequence is that aggressively leaving lane to repeatedly proc the support item on champions no longer accelerates the quest substantially compared with participating normally in Bot lane.

# Support Macro Implications

## Before Level 5

Early roaming has a much larger opportunity cost after Patch 26.16.

Leaving Bot lane can result in:

- 33% reduced minion gold
- 33% reduced minion XP
- reduced lane participation
- slower support quest progression
- increased pressure on the ADC

Therefore do NOT recommend repeatedly leaving Bot lane at level 2-4 without a strong reason.

Good early roam reasons include:

- responding to an immediate fight
- contesting a high-value jungle play
- exploiting a clear roam timer
- moving after the Bot wave is safely handled
- helping on an objective that realistically requires Support

Bad pattern:

spawn
→ permanently roam river / mid / enemy jungle
→ leave ADC isolated for several waves

Patch 26.16 was explicitly designed to make that strategy more costly.

---

# After Level 5 / Later Laning Phase

Roaming becomes less heavily constrained.

Support can more freely:

- establish objective vision
- assist Mid
- move with Jungle
- invade
- control river
- prepare Dragon

but should still consider Bot wave state before leaving.

# Quest-Aware Macro Rules

Do NOT make decisions using quest points alone.

Quest progress should be considered alongside:

- actual gold
- XP
- wave state
- turret plates
- objectives
- champion kills
- death risk
- tempo
- Teleport
- roaming time bank
- team composition

Examples:

"Should Top rotate to Dragon?"

Consider:
- how many waves are lost
- amount of banked roaming time
- whether Dragon is contestable
- Teleport availability
- Top quest completion percentage
- available turret plates
- enemy Top position

"Should ADC roam to Herald?"

Consider:
- number of Bot waves lost
- 3 quest points per lost minion
- Bot plate pressure
- expected Herald value
- probability of winning the fight
- whether ADC presence is actually required

"Should Support roam at level 3?"

Current Season 16 answer should heavily account for the Patch 26.16 penalty:
-33% minion gold and XP outside Bot
until level 5

and the reduced advantage of champion-harass quest stacking.
