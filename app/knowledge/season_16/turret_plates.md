# Season 16 Turrets, Plates, and Crystalline Overgrowth

Last updated: September 2026
Applies to: Standard Summoner's Rift

# Quick Reference

- Turret structures are direct sources of both gold and permanent map pressure, so their value should be compared against objectives, waves, recalls, and rotations.
- Tier 2 turret plate: 120 local gold.
- Tier 3 turret plate: 120 local gold.
- Five plates therefore represent 600 local gold before accounting for the remaining turret reward.
- Inner and inhibitor turret plating can make uncontested side-lane pressure substantially more valuable than simply counting the final turret kill gold.
- Crystalline Overgrowth begins with a 90-second initial windup.
- After activating, Crystalline Overgrowth builds progressively rather than immediately reaching maximum damage.
- At maximum charge, a level-18 Crystalline Overgrowth proc can deal 18.9% of the turret's maximum health.
- Turret pressure should be evaluated using obtainable plate gold + turret gold + waves denied + resulting map access, not turret gold alone.
- Before rotating, estimate how many plates/waves can realistically be secured during the opponent's objective play.
- Do not remain for additional plates if doing so creates a larger loss through death, Baron/Elder, Soul, base exposure, or an unrecoverable numbers disadvantage.
- For exact structure values, Crystalline Overgrowth scaling, resistances, and timing, use the detailed sections below.

# Purpose

Season 16 substantially increased the value of partial turret pressure.

When making macro decisions, turret pressure should be evaluated in terms of:

- immediate plate gold
- full turret gold
- Crystalline Overgrowth damage
- first turret bonus
- wave gold and experience
- enemy resources denied
- objective value being traded away
- time required to complete the push

Do NOT assume rotating to a neutral objective is always better than remaining in lane.

---

# General Turret Rules

All standard lane turrets have 60 Armor and 60 Magic Resistance in the Season 16 system unless another specific modifier applies.

Turrets take 20% increased damage from melee champions.

Turret plate thresholds occur at:

- 10% missing HP
- 25% missing HP
- 45% missing HP
- 70% missing HP
- 100% missing HP

These thresholds correspond to increasingly large health segments.

Crystalline Overgrowth deals true damage, so turret Armor and Magic Resistance do not reduce its damage.

Crystalline Overgrowth cannot trigger while backdoor protection is active.

# Outer Turrets — Tier 1

## Health

Outer turret maximum HP:

9,000

## Plate Gold

Outer turrets have five permanent plates.

Base value:

120 local gold per plate

Maximum local plate gold from a full turret:

600 gold

Unlike previous seasons, plates do NOT disappear at 14:00.

Starting at minute 11, plate value decreases by 10 gold per minute.

Maximum reduction:

40 gold

Therefore the minimum plate value is:

80 gold per plate

Minimum total local plate gold from all five plates:

400 gold

This means early turret pressure is worth more gold than the same turret pressure later.

## Plate Thresholds

Outer plates are claimed at:

- 10% missing HP
- 25% missing HP
- 45% missing HP
- 70% missing HP
- 100% missing HP

Because the thresholds are uneven, later plates require more raw turret damage than earlier plates.

## First Turret

The first turret destroyed in the game grants an additional:

300 gold

This gold is shared among nearby participating allied champions.

Therefore, taking first turret can substantially increase the value of staying in lane instead of rotating elsewhere.

# Inner Turrets — Tier 2

## Health

Inner turret maximum HP:

5,000

## Plate Gold

Inner turret local gold is:

600 gold spread across five plates

Therefore:

120 local gold per plate

Plate thresholds:

- 10% missing HP
- 25% missing HP
- 45% missing HP
- 70% missing HP
- 100% missing HP

Unlike outer turret plates, Riot does not list a time-based reduction for inner turret plate gold.

Therefore each inner turret plate remains worth:

120 local gold

## Full Turret Value

Destroying all five plate segments provides:

600 local gold

The existing global turret bounty is separate from this local plate gold.

A full Tier 2 turret therefore represents a large guaranteed gold conversion, especially for a solo side-laner who receives all local plate gold.

## Macro Implication

A single inner-turret plate is worth 120 guaranteed local gold.

Two plates:

240 gold

Three plates:

360 gold

Four plates:

480 gold

Full turret plate value:

600 gold

This allows direct comparisons against rotations.

For example:

If leaving a side lane for an uncertain fight sacrifices two guaranteed inner-turret plates, the player is giving up approximately 240 local gold before considering:

- the minion wave
- Crystalline Overgrowth
- full turret destruction
- enemy farm denied

The fight therefore needs to have sufficiently high expected value to justify leaving.

# Inhibitor Turrets — Tier 3

## Health

Inhibitor turret maximum HP:

4,750

## Plate Gold

Inhibitor turret local gold is:

600 gold spread across five plates

Therefore:

120 local gold per plate

Plate thresholds:

- 10% missing HP
- 25% missing HP
- 45% missing HP
- 70% missing HP
- 100% missing HP

## Full Turret Value

Five plates provide:

600 local gold

The turret also retains its global team turret bounty.

Therefore destroying an inhibitor turret has value beyond merely opening access to the inhibitor.

## Regeneration

Inhibitor turret regeneration thresholds are:

30% HP
75% HP

This means partial damage can eventually regenerate up to the applicable threshold if the push is abandoned.

This increases the value of completing a meaningful threshold or fully destroying the turret rather than dealing arbitrary damage and leaving.

## Macro Implication

Inhibitor turret pressure is particularly valuable because it creates three forms of value:

1. Local plate gold
2. Access to the inhibitor
3. Permanent map pressure if the inhibitor can subsequently be destroyed

A side-laner taking two inhibitor turret plates earns:

240 local gold

Taking three:

360 local gold

Taking the entire turret:

600 local gold

This can be worth substantially more than joining a low-probability neutral-objective fight.

# Inhibitor

The inhibitor itself is separate from the inhibitor turret.

Destroying an inhibitor grants:

50 gold

to the champion who lands the final hit.

More importantly, destroying the inhibitor causes allied super minions to spawn in that lane while the inhibitor is down.

The strategic value of an inhibitor is therefore much greater than its direct 50-gold reward.

Destroying an inhibitor:

- creates automatic lane pressure
- forces the defending team to answer super minions
- makes Baron and other map plays easier
- can create numbers advantages elsewhere on the map

When evaluating whether to remain and destroy an inhibitor, prioritize the map-pressure value over the direct 50 gold.

# Nexus Turrets

## Health

Each Nexus turret has:

3,500 maximum HP

60 Armor
60 Magic Resistance

Nexus turrets are structurally different from ordinary plate-gold turrets.

They use segmented health thresholds, but should NOT be treated like Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 turrets that provide 120 local gold per segment.

## Gold

Each Nexus turret provides:

50 global gold to each allied champion

Local gold:

0

Therefore destroying one Nexus turret provides approximately:

250 total team gold

in a standard five-player team.

A player does not receive a large individual local payout simply for being the person hitting the Nexus turret.

## Respawn

Nexus turrets respawn after being destroyed.

When they respawn, they return at:

40% maximum HP

rather than full health.

For a 3,500 HP Nexus turret:

40% HP = 1,400 HP

## Regeneration

Nexus turrets regenerate:

6 HP per second

Their current regeneration thresholds are:

40% HP
70% HP

This means a partially damaged Nexus turret can regenerate upward toward its applicable threshold.

## Macro Implication

Unlike a Tier 2 or Tier 3 turret, a Nexus turret's immediate personal gold value is relatively low.

The important value is positional:

- exposing the Nexus
- reducing base defenses
- creating future ending opportunities
- forcing defenders to remain close to their base

Do not recommend taking extreme risks for a Nexus turret purely because of its gold bounty.

Take it when doing so meaningfully increases the probability of ending the game or creating permanent base pressure.

# Crystalline Overgrowth

Crystalline Overgrowth is a Season 16 mechanic designed to reward short pushing windows.

It automatically accumulates on targetable lane turrets.

When an enemy champion attacks a turret with active Overgrowth, the crystals burst and deal bonus true damage.

The damage:

- is based on the turret's maximum HP
- is based on the average level of the attacking team
- does NOT depend on the attacking champion's AD or AP
- does NOT require the champion to remain near the turret
- ignores turret Armor and Magic Resistance because it is true damage

---

# Crystalline Overgrowth Timeline

After an Overgrowth proc is consumed:

## Stage 1 — Startup / Cooldown

Duration:

90 seconds

During this period, Overgrowth cannot be triggered.

At the end of these 90 seconds, Overgrowth becomes available again at its minimum damage value.

---

## Stage 2 — Minimum Damage

Duration:

60 seconds

For the first 60 seconds after Overgrowth becomes available, its damage remains at the minimum value for the attacking team's average level.

Therefore:

90 seconds after the previous proc:
minimum damage becomes available

150 seconds after the previous proc:
the linear growth period begins

---

## Stage 3 — Linear Growth

Duration:

240 seconds

After the 60-second minimum-damage period, Overgrowth damage increases linearly for four minutes.

Therefore:

150 seconds after previous proc:
linear scaling begins

390 seconds after previous proc:
maximum damage is reached

---

# Time Required for Full Crystallization

From the moment a previous Overgrowth proc is consumed:

90 seconds startup
+ 60 seconds minimum state
+ 240 seconds linear growth

= 390 seconds

= 6 minutes 30 seconds

Therefore Crystalline Overgrowth reaches maximum strength:

6 minutes 30 seconds after the previous proc

assuming it is not consumed earlier.

If measuring only from the moment Overgrowth first becomes available after the 90-second startup:

60 + 240 = 300 seconds

= 5 minutes

Therefore it takes five additional minutes after becoming active to reach maximum damage.

Once maximum damage is reached, it remains at maximum strength indefinitely until consumed.

---

# Crystalline Overgrowth Damage

Damage depends on the attacking team's average champion level.

Riot provides the following endpoints.

## Average Team Level 1

Minimum damage:

2.0% of turret maximum HP

Maximum damage:

3.3% of turret maximum HP

## Average Team Level 18

Minimum damage:

8.8% of turret maximum HP

Maximum damage:

18.9% of turret maximum HP

The damage scales between the minimum and maximum state as Overgrowth matures.

---

# Damage Growth Over Time

During the 240-second scaling period, damage increases linearly.

At the beginning of the growth period:

0% of the difference between minimum and maximum damage has been gained.

After 60 seconds of growth:

25% of the difference has been gained.

After 120 seconds:

50%

After 180 seconds:

75%

After 240 seconds:

100%

---

# Example — Level 18 Average Team

At average team level 18:

minimum = 8.8%
maximum = 18.9%

Difference:

10.1 percentage points

Therefore:

90-150 seconds after previous proc:
8.8%

approximately 11.325%

270 seconds after previous proc:
approximately 13.85%

330 seconds after previous proc:
approximately 16.375%

390+ seconds after previous proc:
18.9%

These values assume the attacking team's average level remains at 18 for illustration.

Actual games will usually have a lower and changing average team level.

# Important Overgrowth Interaction

If an enemy unit is close to the turret at the moment the 90-second startup period ends, Crystalline Overgrowth is temporarily suppressed.

It does not appear immediately.

Once enemy units leave, Overgrowth becomes available and fast-forwards to the charge state it would normally have reached.

This prevents Overgrowth from suddenly activating in the middle of an existing siege.

---

# Backdoor Protection

Crystalline Overgrowth does NOT trigger while backdoor protection is active.

Therefore a champion cannot simply walk to an undefended turret without allied minions and use Overgrowth to bypass backdoor protection.

A valid minion-supported push is still required.

---

# Demolish Interaction

Season 16 Demolish is separate from Crystalline Overgrowth.

Demolish triggers on the third consecutive turret attack.

Cooldown:

30 seconds

Melee damage:

85 + 28% of the champion's maximum HP

Ranged damage:

50 + 20% of the champion's maximum HP

Demolish and Crystalline Overgrowth can both contribute to the same pushing window.

Therefore champions using Demolish can generate exceptionally high turret damage during short windows.

# Macro Decision Rule

When comparing a turret push against a rotation, estimate:

Guaranteed Push Value =
plate gold
+ wave gold
+ likely turret destruction value
+ Crystalline Overgrowth progress
+ denied enemy farm
+ strategic structure value

Compare this against:

Expected Rotation Value =
probability of winning the fight
× likely kills / assists / objective rewards
- farm lost
- turret pressure conceded
- death risk

The guaranteed turret play can be superior even when the neutral objective appears more important at first glance.

For example:

If a side-laner can safely take two Tier 2 plates:

240 guaranteed local gold

plus a minion wave

plus Overgrowth damage

while joining Dragon would require abandoning the wave and arriving late to an uncertain fight, staying on the turret may be the higher-value macro decision.

Conversely, guaranteed plate gold should usually not outweigh decisive objectives such as:

- Dragon Soul
- Elder Dragon
- a high-probability Baron
- a fight that can immediately end the game

Always evaluate the complete game state rather than comparing gold alone.
